This project's derivative uses seconds 5.24–6.94 of the recording, resampled to 32 kHz mono PCM. Wind rumble is reduced, high frequencies softened, gain adjusted, and short boundary fades applied. Natural pitch and timing are retained. The derivative is supplied under the same CC0 dedication.
